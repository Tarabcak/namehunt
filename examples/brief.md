# Project brief — working title: "the knowledge engine"

## What we're building
An agentic AI platform that turns a company's scattered internal knowledge —
docs, wikis, tickets, pull requests, chat threads — into a live, queryable
brain. Autonomous agents keep it fresh: they read new material, link related
facts, flag contradictions, and answer questions with sources.

## Who it's for
Mid-size software companies (50–500 people). Buyers are heads of engineering,
platform, and knowledge management.

## Product feel
Calm, precise, trustworthy. It does heavy lifting quietly in the background.
Think "library at midnight", not "rocket launch".

## Naming direction
- Short — ideally 1–2 syllables spoken, easy to say on a sales call.
- Evocative of: memory, connection, weaving, maps, light in dark places, guides.
- Fine with: abstract or mythic words, coined words that sound real.
- Avoid: "-ly"/"-ify" suffixes, "AI"-suffix names, deliberate misspellings,
  anything that sounds like a crypto token.
